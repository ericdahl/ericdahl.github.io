title: 小雨点去旅行
en: Little Raindrop Goes on a Trip
level: 1
voice: zh-CN-XiaoxiaoNeural
rate: -10%
---
天上有一朵白白的云。
There is a fluffy white cloud in the sky.
云里住着很多小雨点。
Lots of little raindrops live inside the cloud.
有一天，一个小雨点说：“我想去下面看看！”
One day, a little raindrop said, "I want to go down and take a look!"

风来了，云变黑了。
The wind came, and the cloud turned dark.
小雨点跳了下去，“哗啦啦，哗啦啦！”
The little raindrop jumped down. "Pitter-patter, pitter-patter!"
它落在一朵红色的花上。
It landed on a red flower.
花儿说：“谢谢你，我正渴呢！”
The flower said, "Thank you, I was so thirsty!"

小雨点从花上滑下来，流进了小河。
The little raindrop slid off the flower and flowed into a little river.
它和小鱼一起游，和小鸭子一起玩。
It swam with the little fish and played with the ducklings.
小河流啊流，流进了大海。
The river flowed and flowed, all the way to the sea.

太阳出来了，天气很热。
The sun came out, and it got very hot.
小雨点觉得自己变轻了，慢慢地飞上了天。
The little raindrop felt itself getting lighter, and slowly it floated up into the sky.
它又回到了白云妈妈的家。
It went back home to Mother Cloud.
小雨点笑着说：“这次旅行真好玩！”
The little raindrop smiled and said, "That was such a fun trip!"
