title: 守株待兔
en: Waiting by the Tree Stump
level: 2
voice: zh-CN-YunxiNeural
rate: -10%
note: A fable from the Han Feizi, about 2,300 years old.
fix: 越长越高=yuè zhǎng yuè gāo

很久很久以前，有一个农夫。
A long, long time ago, there was a farmer.
他每天都在田里干活，从早忙到晚。
He worked in his field every day, busy from morning to night.

有一天，农夫正在田里干活。
One day, the farmer was working in his field.
忽然，一只兔子从草里跑出来。
Suddenly, a rabbit ran out of the grass.
它跑得太快了，一下子撞在了一个树桩上。
It was running so fast that it crashed right into a tree stump.
兔子倒在地上，再也不动了。
The rabbit fell to the ground and didn't move again.

农夫高兴极了。
The farmer was overjoyed.
他想：“不用干活就能得到兔子，太好了！”
He thought, "A rabbit without doing any work — wonderful!"
从那天起，他不再种地了。
From that day on, he stopped working his field.
他每天坐在树桩旁边，等着兔子再来撞树桩。
Every day he sat beside the stump, waiting for another rabbit to run into it.

一天过去了，没有兔子。
A day went by. No rabbit.
十天过去了，还是没有兔子。
Ten days went by. Still no rabbit.
田里的草越长越高，庄稼都死了。
The weeds in his field grew taller and taller, and all his crops died.

农夫什么也没有等到。
The farmer waited, and got nothing at all.
这个故事告诉我们：好东西不会自己从天上掉下来，要靠自己努力。
This story teaches us: good things don't just fall from the sky — we have to work for them.
