en: Tuantuan's New Friend

熊猫团团住在一片大竹林里。
Tuantuan the panda lived in a big bamboo forest.
他黑白分明，圆滚滚的，最爱吃竹子。
He was black and white, round and chubby, and loved eating bamboo more than anything.
可是团团有点儿害羞，不太敢跟别人说话。
But Tuantuan was a little shy, and didn't dare talk to others much.

一天早上，团团正在吃竹子，树上传来一个声音：“你好呀！”
One morning, as Tuantuan was eating bamboo, a voice came from up in a tree: "Hello there!"
团团抬头一看，是一个红棕色的小动物。
Tuantuan looked up and saw a small, reddish-brown animal.
它有一条长长的大尾巴，上面有一圈一圈的花纹。
It had a big, long tail with rings around it.

“你是谁？”团团小声地问。
"Who are you?" Tuantuan asked quietly.
“我是小熊猫！”
"I'm a red panda!" (In Chinese, a red panda is called a "little panda".)
团团吃了一惊：“你也是熊猫？可是你一点儿也不像我呀！”
Tuantuan was startled. "You're a panda too? But you don't look anything like me!"

小熊猫从树上跳下来，哈哈大笑。
The red panda jumped down from the tree, laughing.
“我们的名字很像，可是我们不是一家人。”
"Our names are alike, but we're not from the same family."
“你那么大，我这么小。你是黑白的，我是红色的。你爱睡在地上，我爱睡在树上。”
"You're so big and I'm so small. You're black and white, and I'm red. You like to sleep on the ground, and I like to sleep in trees."

团团想了想，问：“那我们有一样的地方吗？”
Tuantuan thought for a moment and asked, "So is anything about us the same?"
小熊猫拿起一片竹叶，笑着说：“有啊，我们都爱吃竹子！”
The red panda picked up a bamboo leaf and grinned. "Sure — we both love eating bamboo!"

从那天起，团团和小熊猫成了好朋友。
From that day on, Tuantuan and the red panda were good friends.
他们一起吃竹子，一起晒太阳，一起看星星。
They ate bamboo together, lay in the sun together, and watched the stars together.
团团发现，交朋友其实一点儿也不难。
Tuantuan found out that making friends isn't hard at all.
