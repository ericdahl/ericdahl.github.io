title: 十二生肖
en: The Great Zodiac Race
level: 2
voice: zh-CN-XiaoxiaoNeural
rate: -10%
note: A folk tale explaining the order of the twelve animals of the Chinese zodiac.
---
很久很久以前，人们不知道怎么记年份。
A long, long time ago, people didn't know how to keep track of the years.
玉皇大帝想了一个办法：让动物们来比赛。
The Jade Emperor came up with an idea: the animals would have a race.
他说：“最先过河的十二个动物，每个可以代表一年。”
He said, "The first twelve animals to cross the river will each have a year named after them."

猫和老鼠是好朋友。
The cat and the rat were good friends.
猫说：“比赛那天早上，你叫我起床吧。”
The cat said, "On the morning of the race, please wake me up."
老鼠说：“好的。”
The rat said, "Okay."
可是到了那天，老鼠自己悄悄地走了。
But when the day came, the rat quietly left on its own.

河水又宽又急，老鼠不会游泳。
The river was wide and fast, and the rat couldn't swim.
它看见老牛，就跳到了牛的背上。
It saw the ox and jumped onto the ox's back.
老牛游得又稳又快，第一个快要到岸了。
The ox swam steadily and quickly, and was the first to reach the far bank.
就在这时，老鼠从牛背上跳下去，抢了第一名。
Just then, the rat leapt off the ox's back and grabbed first place.

老牛是第二名。
The ox came second.
接着，老虎、兔子、龙、蛇、马、羊、猴子、鸡、狗和猪也都到了。
After that, the tiger, rabbit, dragon, snake, horse, goat, monkey, rooster, dog and pig all arrived too.

猫醒来的时候，比赛已经结束了。
When the cat woke up, the race was already over.
它非常生气，从那天起，猫一看见老鼠就要抓它。
It was furious, and from that day on, whenever a cat sees a rat, it chases it.
你是属什么的呢？
And which animal year were you born in?
