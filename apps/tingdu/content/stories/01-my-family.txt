我叫乐乐。
My name is Lele.
我今年六岁。
I am six years old.
我家有五口人：爸爸、妈妈、奶奶、妹妹和我。
There are five people in my family: Dad, Mom, Grandma, my little sister, and me.
我们家还有一只小白狗，它叫豆豆。
We also have a little white dog. His name is Doudou.

爸爸很高，他每天开车去上班。
Dad is very tall. He drives to work every day.
妈妈是老师，她喜欢唱歌。
Mom is a teacher. She likes to sing.
奶奶会做好吃的饺子。
Grandma knows how to make delicious dumplings.
妹妹两岁了，她最喜欢和豆豆一起玩。
My little sister is two. What she likes best is playing with Doudou.

晚上，我们一起吃饭。
In the evening, we eat dinner together.
吃完饭，爸爸给我们讲故事。
After dinner, Dad tells us stories.
我爱我的家！
I love my family!
