title: 狐假虎威
en: The Fox Borrows the Tiger's Might
level: 3
voice: zh-CN-YunxiNeural
rate: -10%
note: A fable from the Warring States period, over 2,000 years old.
---
森林里住着一只老虎。
A tiger lived in the forest.
有一天，老虎饿了，出去找吃的。
One day the tiger was hungry and went out to look for food.
它抓住了一只狐狸。
It caught a fox.

狐狸心里很害怕，但是它眼珠一转，大声说：
The fox was terrified, but its eyes darted about, and it said loudly:
“你敢吃我？我是天帝派来管理百兽的！”
"You dare to eat me? The Lord of Heaven sent me to rule over all the animals!"
“你要是吃了我，就是不听天帝的话。”
"If you eat me, you'll be disobeying the Lord of Heaven."

老虎有点儿不相信。
The tiger didn't quite believe it.
狐狸说：“不信？你跟在我后面走一走，看看大家怕不怕我。”
The fox said, "You don't believe me? Walk behind me and see whether everyone is afraid of me."

于是，狐狸在前面走，老虎在后面跟着。
So the fox walked in front, and the tiger followed behind.
小鹿看见了，转身就跑。
A little deer saw them, turned, and ran.
兔子看见了，赶快躲进洞里。
A rabbit saw them and hurried into its hole.
猴子看见了，一下子爬到了树上。
A monkey saw them and scrambled up a tree in a flash.

老虎吃惊地说：“原来大家真的都怕你！”
The tiger said in amazement, "So everyone really is afraid of you!"
它放了狐狸，自己也走了。
It let the fox go and went on its way.

可是，动物们怕的到底是谁呢？
But who were the animals really afraid of?
它们怕的不是狐狸，而是狐狸身后的老虎。
Not the fox — but the tiger walking behind it.
