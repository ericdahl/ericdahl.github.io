title: 嫦娥奔月
en: Chang'e Flies to the Moon
level: 3
voice: zh-CN-XiaoxiaoNeural
rate: -10%
note: The legend behind the Mid-Autumn Festival.
---
很久很久以前，天上有十个太阳。
Long, long ago, there were ten suns in the sky.
大地被晒得又干又热，庄稼都枯死了，人们生活得非常辛苦。
The land was baked dry and hot, the crops all withered, and life was very hard for people.

有一个叫后羿的英雄，力气特别大。
There was a hero named Hou Yi, who was incredibly strong.
他拉开神弓，一口气射下了九个太阳。
He drew his magic bow and shot down nine of the suns, one after another.
从此，天气变得凉快了，大家都很感谢他。
After that the weather became cool, and everyone was very grateful to him.

后羿有一个美丽善良的妻子，名叫嫦娥。
Hou Yi had a beautiful, kind-hearted wife named Chang'e.
有一天，王母娘娘送给后羿一包仙药。
One day, the Queen Mother of the West gave Hou Yi a packet of magic elixir.
吃了这种药，就可以飞到天上，成为神仙。
Whoever took this elixir could fly up to the heavens and become immortal.
后羿舍不得离开嫦娥，就把药交给她保管。
Hou Yi couldn't bear to leave Chang'e, so he gave her the elixir to keep safe.

有一个坏人知道了这件事。
A wicked man found out about it.
一天，后羿出门打猎，坏人闯进家里，要嫦娥交出仙药。
One day, while Hou Yi was out hunting, the wicked man burst into the house and demanded that Chang'e hand over the elixir.
嫦娥不愿意把药给坏人，就一口把药吞了下去。
Chang'e would not give it to him, so she swallowed the elixir in one gulp.

嫦娥的身体越来越轻，慢慢地飘出窗外，向天上飞去。
Chang'e grew lighter and lighter, slowly drifted out of the window, and flew up toward the sky.
她舍不得后羿，就停在了离人间最近的月亮上。
She couldn't bear to be far from Hou Yi, so she stopped on the moon, the place closest to the world of people.

后羿回到家，看不见嫦娥，心里非常难过。
When Hou Yi came home and couldn't find Chang'e, he was heartbroken.
他抬头一看，月亮又圆又亮，好像嫦娥在看着他。
He looked up, and the moon was round and bright, as if Chang'e were watching him.
他在院子里摆上嫦娥最爱吃的水果和点心，思念着她。
He laid out Chang'e's favorite fruit and treats in the courtyard, and thought of her.

后来，每年的八月十五，人们都会一边赏月，一边吃月饼。
Ever since, on the fifteenth day of the eighth month, people admire the moon while eating mooncakes.
这一天就是中秋节。
That day is the Mid-Autumn Festival.
