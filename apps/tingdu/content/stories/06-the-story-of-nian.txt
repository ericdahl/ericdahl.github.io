title: 年的故事
en: The Story of Nian
level: 2
voice: zh-CN-XiaoxiaoNeural
rate: -10%
note: A folk legend about why Chinese New Year is celebrated with red, lights and firecrackers.
---
很久以前，有一个怪兽，名字叫“年”。
Long ago, there was a monster called "Nian".
年的头上有角，样子非常可怕。
Nian had horns on its head and looked terrifying.
每年冬天的最后一天，它就跑到村子里吃东西，还会伤害人。
On the last day of every winter, it came to the village to eat, and it hurt people too.
大家都很害怕，一到那天就躲进山里。
Everyone was very afraid, and when that day came they hid in the mountains.

有一年，村子里来了一位白头发的老爷爷。
One year, an old man with white hair came to the village.
他说：“别怕，我有办法赶走年。”
He said, "Don't be afraid. I know how to drive Nian away."

那天晚上，年又来了。
That night, Nian came again.
它看见一家门上贴着红纸，吓了一跳。
It saw red paper pasted on a family's door and got a fright.
这时候，院子里突然“噼里啪啦”地响了起来。
Just then, there was a sudden "crackle-pop!" in the courtyard.
原来是老爷爷在烧竹子！
It was the old man, burning bamboo!
屋子里灯火通明，老爷爷穿着红衣服，哈哈大笑。
The house was brightly lit, and the old man, dressed in red, laughed out loud.
年吓得转身就跑，再也不敢回来了。
Nian was so scared it turned and ran, and never dared to come back.

原来，年最怕红色、火光和响声。
It turned out that what Nian feared most was the color red, firelight, and loud noises.
从那以后，每年的这一天，家家户户都贴红纸、放鞭炮、点灯笼。
Ever since, on this day every year, every family puts up red paper, sets off firecrackers and lights lanterns.
大家还会互相说：“过年好！”
And everyone says to each other, "Happy New Year!"
