星期六早上，奶奶说：“乐乐，跟我去买菜吧！”
On Saturday morning, Grandma said, "Lele, come with me to buy groceries!"
我拿上小篮子，跟着奶奶出门了。
I took my little basket and went out with Grandma.

菜市场里人很多，非常热闹。
The market was full of people and very lively.
有卖菜的，有卖水果的，还有卖鱼的。
There were people selling vegetables, people selling fruit, and people selling fish.

我们先来到卖菜的地方。
First we went to the vegetable stall.
奶奶问：“西红柿多少钱一斤？”
Grandma asked, "How much for a jin of tomatoes?"
阿姨说：“五块钱一斤。”
The lady said, "Five yuan a jin."
奶奶买了两斤西红柿，还买了一把青菜。
Grandma bought two jin of tomatoes, and a bunch of greens too.

然后，我们去买水果。
Then we went to buy fruit.
红红的苹果，黄黄的香蕉，紫紫的葡萄，我都想吃。
Red apples, yellow bananas, purple grapes — I wanted to eat them all.
奶奶笑着说：“只能选一样哦。”
Grandma laughed and said, "You can only pick one."
我想了想，选了葡萄。
I thought about it and picked the grapes.

回家的路上，篮子变得很重。
On the way home, the basket got very heavy.
我说：“奶奶，我来帮你拿！”
I said, "Grandma, let me carry it for you!"
奶奶说：“乐乐长大了，真是个好孩子。”
Grandma said, "Lele is all grown up. What a good child."
