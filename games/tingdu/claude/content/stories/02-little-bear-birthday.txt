今天是小熊的生日。
Today is Little Bear's birthday.
小熊很早就起床了。
Little Bear got up very early.
他想：今天会有朋友来吗？
He wondered: will any friends come today?

“咚咚咚！”有人敲门。
"Knock, knock, knock!" Someone was at the door.
小兔子来了，她送给小熊一个大萝卜。
Little Rabbit came. She gave Little Bear a big radish.
小猴子来了，他送给小熊三个香蕉。
Little Monkey came. He gave Little Bear three bananas.
小鸟也来了，她给小熊唱了一首歌。
Little Bird came too. She sang Little Bear a song.

小熊说：“谢谢你们！可是我没有准备蛋糕。”
Little Bear said, "Thank you! But I didn't make a cake."
大家笑了：“没关系，我们一起做吧！”
Everyone laughed. "That's okay, let's make one together!"
他们用萝卜、香蕉和蜂蜜，做了一个特别的蛋糕。
With the radish, the bananas and some honey, they made a very special cake.

小熊吹灭了蜡烛，许了一个愿望。
Little Bear blew out the candles and made a wish.
他的愿望是什么呢？
What was his wish?
他希望每年都能和好朋友在一起。
He wished he could be with his good friends every year.
